Tyre wear, circuit by circuit, under every estimator.

Switch between the estimators and watch the data change its mind. Only the corrected ones put the softest tyre as the fastest to wear at most circuits, and they were never told that it should.

The naive estimator compares a car with itself across a race, so it confuses tyre wear with the conditions each compound was chosen for. Two-way fixed effects compare cars at the same moment of the same race. The censoring correction then re-weights for stints cut short because the tyre was finished.

The simulator uses the corrected slope pulled toward its compound's average across circuits, by an amount set by how noisy the circuit's own estimate is. That pooling was adopted only because it lowered prediction error on races held out from fitting.
