What this does not establish.

  • Adjacent circuits are not separable; only the extremes are.
  • The per-team and per-driver strategy audit is withheld, because the model failed its own gate.
  • Fuel burn and track evolution can't be separated in race data; they are reported combined.
  • Per-circuit compound wear is noisy: its held-out error is about the size of the effect.
  • Wet races and races stopped early are excluded, by rules written before any result.
