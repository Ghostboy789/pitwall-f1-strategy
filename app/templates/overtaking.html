{{ "{:,}".format(metrics.n_opportunities) }} chances to pass, and what decided them.

Every lap a car ran within reach of the car ahead is an opportunity. Most were never taken. The gap, DRS and a pace advantage decide which ones were.

A pass is counted when the following car is ahead at the end of the lap, on a green lap, without a pit stop explaining it. The overtaking model predicts that outcome and is scored out of fold, one race held out at a time, so it is never graded on races it learned from.

The season and circuit filters re-total the observed rates. The model scores are for all races, as fitted.
