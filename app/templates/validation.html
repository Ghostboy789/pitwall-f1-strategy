V2 · Overtaking calibration

A gradient-boosted model of passing mechanics with a circuit effect shrunk by empirical Bayes, predicting whether a car within reach completes a pass.

AUC {{ "%.3f"|format(metrics.overtaking_auc) }} and Brier {{ "%.4f"|format(metrics.overtaking_brier) }} on a {{ "%.1f"|format(100 * metrics.n_passes / metrics.n_opportunities) }}% base rate. Removing the pace feature, the one most able to leak the outcome, costs 0.015 AUC: the model is not running on a variable that already knows the answer.

V3 · The sanity gate

Real F1 strategists are good. An optimiser that claims to beat them by more than 2 s on average, in more than 70% of car-races, or by more than 30 s in any single one without an identifiable cause, is declared broken.

It failed on all three. The counterfactual audit of teams and drivers is therefore withheld. The failure localises to stop count, which V6 explains.

V6 · Strategy costs, real vs simulated

Added after V3 failed, to find out why: the same cars and races, with the strategy each car actually ran, costed twice.

Real finishing times are insensitive to both how many stops a car made and how it split its stints. The simulator is not. Two calibrated fixes were tried and rejected: each matched one quantity and missed the other.
